HOW IT WILL WORK

Three simple steps.

  1. 1
    Print the tags

    Make a tag for each machine and attach it where the crew can reach it.

  2. 2
    Scan with a phone

    Any crew member scans the tag with their phone’s camera.

  3. 3
    Act on the right record

    See the history or report a problem against the correct equipment.

Questions

Can I buy Equipment QR Tags now?

Not yet. Equipment QR Tags is in development, so there is no price, checkout, or signup for it. It isn’t included in any current plan.

Will it need Maintenance Hub?

QR Tags is designed to open equipment records in Maintenance Hub. Package details will be announced before it can be bought.

Can anyone who scans a tag see our records?

No. A scan asks for Teamstead sign-in first. Equipment records stay behind sign-in and company access.
