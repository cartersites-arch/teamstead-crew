TEAMSTEAD CREW

About Parts Finder

Parts Finder is included in your company’s Teamstead plan. It is not a retailer, manufacturer, marketplace, or inventory system.

How results work

Internet prices and seller-reported online stock come from a protected search service. Nearby-store details come from map search results. Results can be incomplete, delayed, or incorrect.

Before buying

Confirm the part number, dimensions, fit, seller, final price, shipping, returns, and availability with the seller or manufacturer. Local-store inventory is not guaranteed.

Privacy

Your shopping list, saved prices, and store details stay in this browser. Searches are sent through the site server to the search provider. GPS coordinates are used for a nearby-store request and are not saved in the list.

Support

Ask your company owner for Teamstead support or help with account access. Always contact the seller directly about an order, return, price, or stock question.
