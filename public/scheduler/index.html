Questions

Can I buy Preventive Maintenance Scheduler now?

Not yet. Preventive Maintenance Scheduler is in development, so there is no price, checkout, or signup for it. It isn’t included in any current plan.

Where do the intervals come from?

You set them, based on the manufacturer’s instructions and your own requirements. The scheduler does not supply maintenance intervals.

Will it connect to Maintenance Hub?

It is designed to work with Maintenance Hub equipment and history. Package details will be announced before it can be bought.
