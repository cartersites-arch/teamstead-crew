Questions

Can I buy Inspection Checklist Generator now?

Not yet. Inspection Checklist Generator is in development, so there is no price, checkout, or signup for it. It isn’t included in any current plan.

Is it really free?

Yes. It is planned as a free tool with nothing to buy. It isn’t available yet.

Does it tell me what to inspect?

No. You add the checks. The manufacturer’s instructions and any inspection rules that apply to you decide what must be checked.
