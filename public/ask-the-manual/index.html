    Add a manual

    Check the page

Questions

Can I buy Ask the Manual now?

Not yet. Ask the Manual is in development, so there is no price, checkout, or signup for it. It isn’t included in any current plan.

Does it replace the manufacturer’s manual?

No. It helps people find the right part of the manual. The manual and the manufacturer’s instructions are still the authority.

Whose manuals can it read?

Only the manuals your company adds to its own workspace.
